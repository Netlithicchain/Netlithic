Status: the Netlithic proof-of-work testnet is public, but hosted services are not live yet. The plans and prices below are planned and may change before launch.

What to expect

The hosted service is not built yet. Endpoints will follow what the node software actually supports. Today that is reading the chain, status, balances and accounts, and submitting Ed25519-signed transactions. Public plans carry no uptime guarantee at launch. Dedicated uptime terms are agreed per contract once we can measure real uptime. All prices are in US dollars, are not paid in or tied to NLT, and may change before launch.

About these services

Services are paid in US dollars and are separate from NLT. Paying for a service gives you no test coins, no contribution points, and no rights in the network. Netlithic has no venture capital, and service revenue goes toward building and running the network. See the disclaimer.
