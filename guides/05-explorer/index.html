Watch your coins in the explorer

Test network: test coins have no value.

The explorer is a website that lets anyone look at the shared record. It can only look; it can't move coins.

Open https://testnet.netlithicchain.com/explorer/.

What you can see

  • Home: a summary and the latest blocks. A block is a page of the record.
  • A block: its number (height), time, who added it, and its transfers.
  • A transfer: who sent it, who got it, the amount and the fee.
  • An address: its balance and activity.
  • Economy: how the coins are shared out.
  • Search: paste an address, a block number or a transfer ID.

Shortcuts from your wallet

  • See my coins move in the explorer opens your address.
  • Watch this transfer in the explorer appears after you send coins.

A new transfer can take a short while to show up, until it is added to a block.
